# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, provide constructive feedback, and keep discussions focused on improving the project.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, spam, disruptive behavior, and unrelated promotion are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or close comments, issues, and pull requests that violate this code of conduct or fall outside the project's scope. Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project participation.

If you observe unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the project maintainer through an appropriate repository contact channel. Reports will be reviewed in good faith and handled as confidentially as practical.
